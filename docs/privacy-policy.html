Introduction

Fireflies Labs (“we”, “us”) develops Cloksy (“the app”). Your work routine, hours and location are your own business, so Cloksy is built to work without an account and without a server.

This policy describes the information the app handles and how it is protected.

Data stays on your device

Cloksy has no sign-up, username or password. The following information is stored only in a private database on your phone:

InformationWhy the app keeps it
Punch-in and punch-out timesTo calculate worked hours
Monthly targets, day statuses, notesTo show progress and your calendar
Work location coordinates and radiusTo set up the optional geofence
Salary settings (optional)To estimate earnings
Your name (optional)To print on exported PDF reports

We do not operate servers that hold your personal information. We do not collect, sell or share your time data with employers, advertisers or any other third party.

Location and geofencing

If you turn on location-based punching, Cloksy asks for location permission, including background location (“Allow all the time”), so it can notice when you arrive at or leave a workplace you added.

How background location is used Cloksy registers a virtual boundary (geofence) around your workplace using Google Play Services. Android then notifies Cloksy when your device crosses it, even if the app is closed, so Cloksy can prompt you or record your punch.
  • Cloksy does not record a history of where you go, and it does not track you outside your configured work areas.
  • Cloksy has no server and does not upload your coordinates.
  • The “use current location” button and address lookup use Android’s own location and geocoding services, which may contact Google’s services under Google’s privacy policy. Cloksy only fills the result into the form on your phone.
  • Location permission is optional. If you decline, you can still punch in and out manually.

Salary tracking

Salary tracking is off by default. If you turn it on, the take-home pay and paid-leave count you enter are used only on your phone to estimate earnings.

Estimates only Earnings shown in Cloksy are for personal planning. They are not payroll records, accounting, or legal advice.

Third parties and analytics

Cloksy contains no advertising SDKs, analytics frameworks, or trackers from ad networks, data brokers or social platforms. The only Google component involved is Google Play Services, which provides the geofencing and location features described above.

Your control

  • Export: create a monthly A4 PDF timesheet whenever you like. It is shared only to the app you choose.
  • Edit or delete: change or remove punch records and day statuses from the calendar.
  • Erase everything: go to Settings → Apps → Cloksy → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Withdraw location access: change Cloksy’s location permission in Android Settings at any time.

Privacy laws

Because your information stays on your device and is not sent to us, Cloksy is designed to minimize data collection in line with principles found in the GDPR (European Union), LGPD (Brazil) and the DPDP Act (India). If you export a PDF and share it, that copy is under your control.
